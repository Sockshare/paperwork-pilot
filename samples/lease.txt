RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

Maplewood Properties Management, LLC
1200 Commerce Street, Suite 400
Springfield, IL 62701

THIS LEASE is made on August 15, 2026 between Maplewood Properties Management, LLC ("Landlord") and Jordan Rivera ("Tenant") for the premises located at 418 Birch Lane, Apartment 3B, Springfield, IL 62704 (the "Premises").

1. TERM. The lease begins on September 1, 2026 and ends on August 31, 2027. This Lease shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the current term.

2. RENT. Tenant shall pay rent of $1,450.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month. Rent received after the 5th day of the month is subject to a late fee of $75.00 plus $10.00 per day until paid in full. Upon renewal, rent may be increased by up to 6% with thirty (30) days written notice.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,450.00 before move-in. The deposit will be returned within 30 days after move-out, less any deductions for damage beyond normal wear and tear, unpaid rent, or cleaning. A non-refundable cleaning fee of $250.00 is also due at signing.

4. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for electricity, gas, internet and cable. Landlord pays water, sewer and trash.

5. EARLY TERMINATION. If Tenant terminates this Lease before the end of the term, Tenant shall pay an early termination fee equal to two (2) months' rent and forfeit the security deposit, unless a replacement tenant approved by Landlord takes over the Lease.

6. PETS. No pets are allowed without written permission. An approved pet requires a pet deposit of $400.00 and pet rent of $35.00 per month.

7. RIGHT OF ENTRY. Landlord may enter the Premises with 24 hours notice for repairs, inspections, or showings, and without notice in an emergency.

8. SUBLETTING. Tenant may not sublet the Premises or assign this Lease without Landlord's prior written consent.

9. MAINTENANCE. Tenant must report needed repairs in writing. Tenant is responsible for repairs costing less than $100.00 caused by Tenant's use. Tenant must replace smoke detector batteries and furnace filters.

10. PARKING. One assigned parking space (#14) is included. Additional spaces are $50.00 per month.

11. RENTER'S INSURANCE. Tenant must maintain renter's insurance with at least $100,000 in liability coverage for the entire term and provide proof within 14 days of move-in.

12. DISPUTES. Any dispute arising under this Lease shall be resolved by binding arbitration in Sangamon County, Illinois. Tenant waives the right to a jury trial and to participate in any class action.

13. MOVE-OUT. Tenant must give written notice at least 60 days before moving out. Tenant must return all keys and leave the Premises clean. A re-key fee of $125.00 applies if any key is not returned.

14. GOVERNING LAW. This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.

Please sign and return this Lease with the security deposit and cleaning fee by August 25, 2026 to hold the apartment.
